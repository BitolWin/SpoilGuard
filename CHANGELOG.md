# SpoilGuard · Release notes

_Versión en español: [CHANGELOG.es.md](CHANGELOG.es.md)_

## 1.5 · 2026-11-18

**🧠 Engine & protection**

- SG Engine V1: much faster filtering, and up to 15,000 words per list
- “Pause just this once”: uncovers only the current page until you reload it
- Images are also blocked by their file name, even when the word is hidden (optional)
- Optional: the notice says where the word was found (title, comment, chat, file name…)
- “Only titles” mode and a minimum text length for fewer false positives
- Protection schedule: protect only between the hours you choose
- Turn SpoilGuard off on the current site from the window

**⚡ Performance**

- Video players are left completely alone: smoother YouTube and Twitch
- Lite mode for modest computers: no animations, shadows or blurs (turns on by itself on very modest machines)
- Smoother scrolling in the small window, especially with a custom background
- Lighter on heavy sites like X and Twitch, and the window opens faster

**📝 Lists & editor**

- Undo arrow in the editor
- “Wasn’t a spoiler?”: add an exception in one click when you reveal something
- The protection level now counts your settings, with new ranks
- Templates for new lists, and name suggestions from any text you paste
- Turn a list off on specific sites, and a counter for each list
- Drag your lists on Home to reorder them
- Release mode for each list: switches off a few days after release
- Editor: search with highlighting, test a phrase, duplicate list and undo

**🎨 Appearance**

- Your own background image for the window, and another one for the notices
- Adjust the size and position of your images
- Paste an image with Ctrl+V without leaving the small window
- Shrink your images down to 25%, with a photo frame in the theme colour or the image’s own colours
- Precision sliders: the slower you move, the finer the adjustment
- Our own colour picker and smoother sliders that follow the mouse
- Custom theme colour and a new colour picker

**🧭 Guides & comfort**

- Everything is saved before the notes or the privacy policy open
- Welcome page on install, with a ready-made GTA 6 list
- New guides: a 1-minute quick guide, a GTA 6 one and the full tour in on-screen order
- Clear warning about Chrome’s address bar, with an example and how to avoid it
- Floating window: open SpoilGuard in a window you can move anywhere on the screen
- Clearer guides: easier-to-read example, centred buttons and a timeless example outside the GTA 6 guide
- The Chrome address bar warning can be folded and still shows the warning
- “Back” button on these pages, and find a language by typing its name in any language
- The 📜 icon shows how many notes you haven’t read
- Reset the counter and a “Send suggestion” button

**🔒 Privacy & data**

- Full backup of your lists and settings
- This patch notes page and a notice when the privacy policy changes

**🌍 Languages**

- New languages: 繁體中文, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, हिन्दी, ไทย, বাংলা, Українська, Filipino, Bahasa Melayu, Română

**🛠️ Fixes**

- Channel profile pictures are no longer covered
- “Send suggestion” now opens SpoilGuard’s support page in the store
